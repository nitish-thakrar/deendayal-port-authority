About Deendayal Port Authority

Our Connectivity
A strategically located all-weather tidal port with strong rail, road and air connectivity from the heart of Kandla Creek.

Deendayal Port is an all-weather natural tidal port situated in Kandla Creek. It is located 90 km inside from the mouth of Gulf of Kachchh and well connected with Rail, Road and Air.

Deendayal Port Authority, Kandla, one of the twelve Major Ports of India, has scripted a historic milestone during the financial year 2025–26. The port handled an impressive total of 3,960 vessels and achieved a record-breaking cargo throughput of 160.11 Million Metric Tonnes. With this remarkable performance, DPA secured the Numero-Uno position among all Major Ports in the country and proudly became the first Major Port in India to surpass the landmark figure of 160 Million Tonnes in cargo handling.

With a total capacity of 263 MTPA and 35 jetties handling various cargo types, including liquid, dry bulk, and containers, we are further planning to increase our current handling capacity by appx. 50% to 392 MTPA by inviting private players to operate cargo and oil jetties through Public Private Partnership mode.

Salient Features & Green Initiatives

Green Hydrogen Plant

DPA becomes the first port in India to commission a Mega-Watt scale Green Hydrogen Plant in July 2025, with an additional 5 MW electrolyser order placed in January 2026.

Location: Kandla Port

Commissioned: July 2025

Capacity: 0.5 Tons per day

India’s First Green Tug

Under the government’s Green Tug Transition Programme, DPA Kandla has placed an order for India’s first all-electric green tug with 60-ton bollard pull capacity.

Bollard Pull: 60 Ton

Emissions: Zero Carbon

Operation: Silent with minimal vibration

Automated Cargo Evacuation

Fast cargo evacuation using a magnetic levitation-based transportation solution for reliable, next-generation port handling.

Performance: Fast cargo evacuation

Technology: Magnetic levitation
